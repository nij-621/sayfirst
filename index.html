What this trains

  1. Point first — the conclusion, question, or ask is in the first sentence
  2. Framing — the listener knows why they're hearing this before the details
  3. Clear references — no bare "this / it / that stuff"; names, not pointers
  4. Clean sentences — one planned sentence at a time, no mid-sentence restarts

Grading and rewrite: one round, about three minutes. Do it before you walk over to someone.
