How a session works

  1. Enter one real thing you have to say: situation, listener, what you want
  2. Speak first — 5 seconds to think, up to 45 to talk. No typing, no hints
  3. Name the biggest problem yourself, then get the shape for this kind of message
  4. Say it again. The coach gives one fix, not ten
  5. Say it a last time with everything hidden. Model line only if you ask
  6. Two days later: same shape, new situation. After the real meeting: did anyone ask "so what do you need?"
